# Brief -- map icm-architect

## What is this for

A viewer who has never heard of ICM should be able to say, in ninety seconds,
what icm-architect is for, what it does when somebody asks it to structure their
work, and what every result is checked against -- from the map alone.

## Who is watching

Somebody deciding whether to point this skill at their own folder, or a model
opening the workspace cold. Afterwards they should open `catalog.md`, then one
card.

## What it must say

Every line is already a claim on a card in `example-map/icm-architect/`:

- The root `CLAUDE.md` is 18 lines and names SKILL.md and `references/`
  (`CLAUDE.md:8-9`); finished work goes to `blueprints/` (`CLAUDE.md:11`).
- `SKILL.md` carries ten invariants (`SKILL.md:14-27`), the two modes, and the
  walk test (`SKILL.md:85-97`): an agent with no memory must orient, act and
  report status from the files alone.
- `references/forms.md` holds six forms, chosen by the repeating unit of work
  (`references/forms.md:7-18`). `references/core.md` holds the five principles a
  contract is checked against (`references/core.md:7-17`).
- `assets/templates/` holds eight blank starters, copied out rather than edited,
  per invariant ten (`SKILL.md:27`).
- Two files here are called `CLAUDE.md`, and only one of them is the door
  (`collisions.md`).

## What it must not do

No adjectives about how good the method is. No causes. No number that is not on
a card.
