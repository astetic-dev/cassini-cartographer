# Species naming standard

How a species is written anywhere in this workspace: in the tally, in the return,
in the post.

## The form

English name, lower case, no hyphen unless the accepted name has one. Willow
warbler, not Willow Warbler and not willow-warbler.

Scientific name only where two species share an English name in this county, and
then in brackets after it, italic.

## Age and sex

Age uses the scheme's codes: `1J`, `2`, `3`, `4`, `5`, `6`. Never "juvenile" in a
table; "juvenile" is allowed in the post.

Sex is `M`, `F` or `U`. Never blank.

## Groups

Where a run cannot separate two species in the hand, write the pair the scheme
writes: "willow warbler / chiffchaff". Never invent a group name.

## Order

Species appear in the order the log has them, which is the order they came out of
the nets. Not alphabetical, and not by count.
