# House voice

How the station post reads. One page, for people who walk past the station and
want to know what was in the nets.

## Register

Plain and flat. The post is a record of a week, not an appeal.

Open with the bird, not with the week. "A juvenile redstart came out of net four
on Thursday" is the opening. "This week at the station" is not.

## Numbers

Every count is a figure, including counts under ten. A count without a figure is
cut.

Totals go next to the species, never in a closing summary.

## Names

Ringers appear as first name only. Visitors appear in full on first mention.

## Length

Between 200 and 500 words. Between one and three headings.

## What is not in a post

No ring numbers. No net positions. No call to action, and no thanks for reading.
